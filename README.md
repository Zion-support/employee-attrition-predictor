# Employee Attrition Predictor

Employee attrition risk prediction, retention playbooks and engagement signals.

**Live app:** https://ziontechgroup.com/employee-attrition-predictor/

> **Status:** This repository documents a product concept and its landing page. A published page is not proof of a functioning AI engine; verify the implementation before a pilot.

## What the concept covers
- Flight-risk scoring per employee with explainable drivers
- Retention playbooks triggered by risk tier
- Engagement survey signal fusion (eNPS, pulses, 1:1s)
- Cost-of-attrition modeling for CFO-ready business cases
